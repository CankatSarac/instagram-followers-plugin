## Overview

Instagram Unfollower Detective is a Chrome extension designed to help Instagram users identify accounts they follow that don't follow them back. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data with complete transparency.

## Data Collection

**WE DO NOT COLLECT ANY PERSONAL DATA**

Our extension operates entirely within your browser and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

### What We Access (Locally Only)
- **Instagram follower/following lists**: Accessed only through Instagram's public APIs while you're logged in
- **Profile information**: Basic public profile data (usernames, profile pictures) for display purposes only
- **Browser storage**: Small amounts of local data for extension preferences and temporary results

### What We DO NOT Access
- ❌ Your Instagram password or login credentials
- ❌ Your personal messages or private content
- ❌ Your browsing history outside of Instagram
- ❌ Your location or device information
- ❌ Your financial or payment information
- ❌ Your contacts or personal communications

## Data Processing

### Local Processing Only
- All data analysis happens locally in your browser
- Instagram follower data is processed temporarily and never sent to external servers
- Results are displayed immediately and can be cleared at any time
- No data is stored permanently on your device without your explicit action

### Temporary Storage
- **Local Browser Storage**: Used to store user preferences (whitelist, filter settings)
- **Session Data**: Temporary follower/following data during analysis (automatically cleared)
- **No External Storage**: No data is ever transmitted to or stored on external servers

## Data Sharing

**WE DO NOT SHARE ANY DATA**

- ❌ No data is shared with third parties
- ❌ No data is sold or monetized
- ❌ No analytics or tracking services are used
- ❌ No advertising networks have access to your data

## Permissions Explanation

Our extension requests minimal permissions necessary for functionality:

### activeTab
- **Purpose**: Access the current Instagram tab when you click the extension
- **Usage**: Read Instagram page content to identify follower relationships
- **Limitation**: Only works on the active tab, only when you initiate the action

### storage
- **Purpose**: Save your preferences locally (whitelist, settings)
- **Usage**: Remember which accounts you've whitelisted between sessions
- **Location**: Stored locally in your browser only

### scripting
- **Purpose**: Inject the detective interface into Instagram pages
- **Usage**: Display the unfollower analysis interface on Instagram.com
- **Limitation**: Only executes on Instagram domains when you activate the extension

### Host Permission (https://www.instagram.com/*)
- **Purpose**: Access Instagram's public API endpoints for follower data
- **Usage**: Retrieve public follower/following information using Instagram's standard APIs
- **Limitation**: Only accesses public data that's already visible on Instagram

## Data Security

### Security Measures

## Third-Party Services

## Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Instagram's own terms require users to be at least 13 years old.

## International Users
