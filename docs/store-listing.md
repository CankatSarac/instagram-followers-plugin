# Instagram Detective — proposed listing

Compare your Instagram exports privately.

See which accounts in your following export are absent from your followers export. Download your Instagram information as JSON, unzip it, and import followers and following. Include all numbered follower files for a complete comparison.

- Compare multiple export files and remove duplicate usernames.
- View accounts that do not follow back, with follower, following and mutual counts.
- Download the complete results as CSV.
- Clear imported files and results whenever you choose.

Your files are processed locally in this tab. No Instagram login, password, cookies, account access, or automated follow/unfollow actions are required. Close the comparison tab or choose Clear data to discard imported data.

Results reflect the files you import. Incomplete or older exports can change the result. This does not identify historical unfollow events from a single snapshot. The interface displays up to 1,000 accounts; CSV includes the full results.
